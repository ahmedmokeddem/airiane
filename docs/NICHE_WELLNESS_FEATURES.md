## 1. Acoustic, Vibroacoustic & Auditory Neuromodulation

1. **Multisensory 40 Hz Gamma Entrainment (GENUS: Audio + Visual)**
   - **Papers:**
     - Martorell et al. (2019), *"Multi-sensory Gamma Stimulation Ameliorates Alzheimer's-Associated Pathology and Improves Cognition"*, **Cell**.
     - Adaikkan et al. (2019), *"Gamma Entrainment Binds Higher-Order Brain Regions and Reduces Pathology"*, **Neuron**.
     - Jirakittayakorn & Wongsawat (2017), *"Brain responses to 40-Hz binaural beat and effects on emotion and memory"*, **Frontiers in Neuroscience**.
   - **Why it matters:** Combining **40 Hz amplitude-modulated nature audio** with **40 Hz pulsing LED light** (on the Arduino Bedside NeuroPod) drives gamma synchrony across auditory, visual, hippocampal, and prefrontal networks far more broadly than audio or light alone.

2. **Finnish 40 Hz Vibroacoustic Therapy (VAT) — *Local Academic Goldmine!***
   - **Papers:**
     - Punkanen & Ala-Ruona (2012), *"Contemporary Vibroacoustic Therapy: Perspectives on Clinical Practice, Research, and Training"*, **Music and Medicine** (University of Jyväskylä / VIBRAC Centre, Finland).
     - Campbell, Hynynen, Burger, Vainionpää, & Ala-Ruona (2021), *"Vibroacoustic treatment to improve functioning and ability to work: a multidisciplinary approach to chronic pain rehabilitation"*.
   - **Why it matters:** Low-frequency pulsed sinusoidal vibration (centered around **40 Hz**) delivered through tactile transducers or phone haptics calms autonomic hyperarousal and comorbid pain/stress. Citing Finnish University of Jyväskylä research at Junction (Aalto) resonates strongly with local judges.

3. **Biophilic Acoustic Grounding (Default Mode Network Down-Regulation)**
   - **Paper:** Gould van Praag et al. (2017), *"Mind-wandering and alterations to default mode network connectivity when listening to naturalistic versus artificial sounds"*, **Scientific Reports** (*Nature*), 7, 45273.
   - **Mechanism:** Naturalistic sounds (forest birds, rain, streams) shift autonomic tone to parasympathetic dominance (high-frequency HRV) and shift Default Mode Network (DMN) connectivity outward, interrupting rumination.

4. **Bilateral Alternating Auditory Stimulation (BLS)**
   - **Papers:**
     - Schubert et al. (2014), *"Facilitating Access to Emotions: Neural Signature of EMDR Stimulation"*, **PLoS ONE**.
     - Rousseau et al. (2020), *"Cracking the EMDR Code: Recruitment of Sensory, Memory and Emotional Networks During Bilateral Alternating Auditory Stimulation"*.
   - **Mechanism:** Slow 0.5–1.0 Hz stereo left-right panning modulates amygdala–DLPFC connectivity, dampening emotional hyperarousal.

5. **1/f Pink Noise for Restorative Slow-Wave Sleep**
   - **Paper:** Ngo, Martinetz, Born, & Mölle (2013), *"Auditory closed-loop stimulation of the sleep slow oscillation enhances memory"*, **Neuron**.
   - **Mechanism:** Gentle pink noise ($1/f$ equal energy per octave) supports deep slow-wave oscillations (<1 Hz) without jarring the auditory cortex.

## 2. Visual, Photobiological & Thermal Interventions

6. **Narrow-Band 525 nm Green Light Therapy (Autonomic & Pain/Anxiety Calm)**
   - **Paper:** Ibrahim et al. (2017), *"Long-lasting antinociceptive effects of green light in acute and chronic pain in rats"*, **Pain** (and subsequent human migraine/fibromyalgia trials).
   - **Mechanism:** Visual exposure to narrow-band **525 nm green LED light** engages descending inhibitory circuits and endogenous opioid pathways, reducing sympathetic overdrive and anxiety without photophobia.
   - **Hackathon build:** Add a `"525 nm Calm Green"` mode to the **Arduino RGB LED Ring**!

7. **Morning Melanopsin ipRGC Activation & Evening Blue Attenuation**
   - **Paper:** Lam et al. (2016), *"Efficacy of Bright Light Treatment, Fluoxetine, and the Combination in Patients With Nonseasonal Major Depressive Disorder"*, **JAMA Psychiatry**, 73(1), 56–63.
   - **Mechanism:** Morning ~480 nm light stimulates melanopsin ipRGCs to reset circadian phase delay; evening zero-blue amber preserves melatonin.

8. **Mid-Range Fractal Visuals ($D \approx 1.3–1.5$)**
   - **Papers:**
     - Hägerhäll, Laike, Taylor, Küller, & Martin (2008), *"Investigations of human EEG response to viewing fractal patterns"*, **Perception**, 37(10), 1488–1494.
     - Taylor (2021), *"The Potential of Biophilic Fractal Designs to Promote Health and Performance"*, **Sustainability**.
   - **Mechanism:** Viewing organic fractals with fractal dimension $D \approx 1.3–1.5$ increases frontal EEG alpha waves and reduces skin-conductance physiological stress by up to **60%**.

9. **Whole-Body / Passive Thermal Warming (Sauna & Infrared Rituals)**
   - **Paper:** Janssen, Lowry, Raison, et al. (2016), *"Whole-Body Hyperthermia for the Treatment of Major Depressive Disorder: A Randomized Clinical Trial"*, **JAMA Psychiatry**.
   - **Mechanism:** A single controlled thermal warming session produced rapid mood improvements lasting up to 6 weeks via thermosensory-serotonergic pathways.
   - **Wellness framing:** Suggesting a warm evening bath, warm shower, or Finnish sauna ritual as a zero-effort evening wind-down habit.

## 3. Somatic, Respiratory, Eye-Movement & Olfactory Bypasses

10. **Lateral Eye Movements & Working-Memory Amygdala Deactivation**
    - **Paper:** de Voogd, Kanen, Neville, Roelofs, Fernández, & Hermans (2018), *"Eye-movement intervention enhances extinction via amygdala deactivation"*, **The Journal of Neuroscience**, 38(40), 8694–8707.
    - **Mechanism:** Goal-directed lateral eye movements activate a dorsal frontoparietal network that **transiently deactivates the amygdala** by taxing working memory, taking the emotional sting out of intrusive rumination.
    - **Hackathon build:** A 60-second visual dot moving smoothly left-to-right across the screen synchronized with our 0.5 Hz bilateral stereo audio!

11. **Acoustic Humming / Bhramari (15x Nasal Nitric Oxide & Vagal Vibration)**
    - **Paper:** Weitzberg & Lundberg (2002), *"Humming Greatly Increases Nasal Nitric Oxide"*, **American Journal of Respiratory and Critical Care Medicine** (Karolinska Institutet, Sweden).
    - **Mechanism:** Humming during exhalation increases paranasal nitric oxide (NO) **15-fold**, creating gentle mechanical vagal vibration and parasympathetic calm—and can be detected passively by our existing microphone!

12. **Cyclic Sighing (Physiological Sigh)**
    - **Paper:** Balban, Neri, Kogon, Spiegel, & Huberman (2023), *"Brief structured respiration practices enhance mood and reduce physiological arousal"*, **Cell Reports Medicine**, 4(1), 100895.
    - **Mechanism:** Double nasal inhale + extended exhale outperformed mindfulness meditation in improving daily positive affect and lowering resting respiratory rate.

13. **0.1 Hz Resonance Frequency Pacing**
    - **Papers:** Pizzoli et al. (2021), **Scientific Reports**, 11, 6614; Caldwell & Steffen (2018), **International Journal of Psychophysiology**.
    - **Mechanism:** ~6 breaths/min (4.5s in / 5.5s out) maximizes Respiratory Sinus Arrhythmia and baroreflex resonance.

14. **Trigeminal-Vagal Cold Face Reset (Mammalian Dive Reflex)**
    - **Mechanism:** Cold stimulus ($<10^\circ\text{C}$) on forehead/upper cheeks activates trigeminal nerve ($V_1/V_2$) $\to$ brainstem $\to$ vagus nerve, causing immediate heart-rate deceleration during acute overwhelm.

15. **Proustian Olfactory Grounding (Direct Limbic Bypass)**
    - **Paper:** Young et al. (2024), *"Recall of Autobiographical Memories Following Odor vs Verbal Cues Among Adults With Major Depressive Disorder"*, **JAMA Network Open**, 7(2), e2356001.
