## Remaining visual and verification gates

The implementation follows the original composition and palette, but is not a pixel-identical reproduction. Native controls/text are larger, reports extend beyond the original mock, the avatar is a fallback, and the current catalogue is intentionally smaller. No animation FPS, release-device performance, iOS, smallest-screen, 200% text, keyboard or full accessibility certification is claimed. Screenshots demonstrate rendered states; they do not establish a successful backend mutation, consent enforcement, camera network isolation or Bluetooth microphone selection. See [implementation status](IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md) for separate verified integration receipts and remaining blockers.
